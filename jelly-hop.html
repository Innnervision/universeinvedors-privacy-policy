Jelly Hop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes how the Jelly Hop Android game, package com.universeinvedors.jellyhop, handles information. Jelly Hop is designed for children. The game developer is Universe Invedors.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a local player profile and game state, which may include an app-generated player ID and display name, avatar choice, scores, level and achievement progress, virtual coins and gems, unlocked game items, match statistics, and sound, control, and display preferences. This information is stored in the app's local database and preferences. The current build does not upload this profile or gameplay history to a Jelly Hop game server, and it does not provide functional Google account sign-in or cloud save synchronization.

Multiplayer lobbies and leaderboard examples in the current build are simulated or stored locally. If you choose to share a lobby invitation, Android opens its share sheet and passes the invitation text to the app you select; Jelly Hop does not receive information from that app.

Advertising and consent services

Jelly Hop uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request ads and present consent choices. These services may process information such as the app and device configuration, IP address, advertising requests and interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. Google and participating ad partners may also process location signals, including location information where permitted by the relevant settings, consent, and partner practices. The app itself does not request Android location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions and does not access GPS location.

Because Jelly Hop is child-directed, ad requests are configured for child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment and limited to a G content rating. Google documents that child-directed treatment disables interest-based and remarketing ads and prevents transmission of the Android advertising ID. Ad delivery still involves information needed to make and measure an ad request. Ads are not initialized unless UMP allows an ad request. You can change available privacy choices from the game's Account settings when UMP indicates that privacy options are required.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the information about Google's partner sites and apps.

Children and parental choices

Jelly Hop is intended for children. The game does not require a child to create an account or provide a real name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Child-directed ad treatment and a G content rating are applied to ad requests. Parents and guardians can contact the developer below with questions or requests concerning information handled by the game. Information held only on a device can be removed by clearing Jelly Hop's app storage or uninstalling the app. Information handled by Google or its ad partners is subject to their own privacy controls and policies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local profile and gameplay information remains on the device until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Android device backup and transfer behavior is controlled by the device and operating-system settings. Jelly Hop has no first-party game server from which to retrieve or delete local game records. Google and its partners set their own retention periods for advertising and consent data.

Security and changes

Local game information is kept in the app's private storage. Network communications for advertising and consent are handled by Google SDKs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy will be updated if Jelly Hop adds accounts, cloud saves, online multiplayer, analytics, or other data-processing features.
