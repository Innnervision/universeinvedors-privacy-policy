Information an app may handle

Depending on an app's features, information may include:

  • App and device information, such as app version, device model, operating system, language, network information, IP address, and app-generated identifiers.
  • App activity, such as gameplay progress, scores, preferences, feature usage, and interactions with ads.
  • Information that you choose to provide, such as a profile name, support message, account details, or text sent to an AI feature.
  • Advertising, consent, diagnostics, and crash information when the app integrates the corresponding SDKs.
  • Purchase or entitlement information when an app offers purchases through Google Play. Google Play processes payment details; the app does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Approximate location that a service may infer from an IP address. An app processes precise location only if that app provides a location feature and requests the required permission.

Some apps store profiles, settings, or progress on the device. Apps that provide accounts or cloud features may send relevant information to their service providers. Information entered into an AI feature may be sent to that feature's model provider to produce a response. Avoid including sensitive personal information in optional prompts or messages.

Advertising, consent, and other service providers

Some apps display ads and use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where integrated, Google and participating ad partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device configuration, ad requests and interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. Whether ads are personalized depends on the app, region, age treatment, and consent or device settings. Available privacy choices can be managed through the app's privacy-options control when provided, and through Google's and Android's privacy settings.

Other apps may use Google or Firebase services for features such as app integrity, analytics, cloud storage, authentication, AI, or crash reporting. A provider receives information needed for the service that the particular app enables. Payment services are handled by Google Play when purchases are offered. Service providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Vanguard: Sovereign Conquest and Dead City 3D: Zombie Survival are intended for users aged 13 and older and are not targeted to children under 13. Other apps may have different audiences and service configurations, which are set for each product.

How information is used and shared

Information is used, where relevant to an app, to provide its features, save progress, maintain accounts, display and measure ads, manage consent, diagnose errors, protect services, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. Information is shared with the service providers described above when needed to provide an enabled feature. An app's exact behavior depends on its current version and permissions.

Children and families

Intended audiences differ by app and are stated in the applicable store listing. Jelly Hop is child-directed and uses child-directed ad request settings. Vanguard: Sovereign Conquest and Dead City 3D: Zombie Survival are intended for ages 13+ and are not targeted to children under 13. For questions about a child-directed app, a parent or guardian may contact us using the address below.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Information stored only on a device generally remains there until you clear that app's storage or uninstall it. When an app uses an account or cloud provider, information may be retained by that provider according to the app's feature and the provider's policies. You may contact us to request assistance with information associated with an app. Advertising and consent providers control retention of information they process and provide their own privacy controls.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by our apps. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. App and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their own safeguards and legal obligations.

Your choices and policy changes

You can use privacy choices available in an app, adjust Android or Google advertising settings, and control app permissions through Android settings. This policy may be updated when products or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
