How we report progress

  • “Staged” means a candidate is stored locally for further verification; it is not publicly downloadable here.
  • “Under review” means a submission is pending Google Play review, not approved or live.
  • “Production” means an existing Console record or track state only when that is the evidence being cited; it does not certify the current local source build.
  • We do not publish unreleased app bundles, signing materials, private credentials, or account-only documents on this status page.
